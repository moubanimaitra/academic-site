Courses Taught

Critical Perspectives on Urban India

Course Curator & Lecturer · Fall 2026

Course Description

This course offers a critical, ethnographically grounded introduction to urban India, examining the social, political, and economic dynamics that produce inequality and exclusion in Indian cities. Key themes include the formal-informal divide, informal settlements, land rights and housing, precarious labour and the gig economy, welfare politics, the urban middle class, and questions of environment, gender, and citizenship. Drawing on case studies from Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru, the course situates these debates within specific political economy contexts while enabling comparative insight across urban India. It is suited to students in urban studies, anthropology, sociology, development studies, and social policy.
